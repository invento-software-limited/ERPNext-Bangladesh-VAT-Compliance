## [2.2.0] - 2026-10-20

### ✨ Added
- Supplier Mushak 6.3 report: a **Total VAT** column (the bill's `total_taxes_and_charges`) before Grand Total.

### 🔧 Changed
- Mushak 6.3 and Supplier Mushak 6.3: wider columns, mostly for customer, company, supplier and amount columns that were cut off.
- VAT Dashboard: the period buttons use Frappe's button styling, and **Custom** opens a panel with a Frappe Date Range field, placed outside the dashboard's shadow root so the desk datepicker works. It closes on Cancel, Escape, an outside click or a route change.

### 🐞 Fixed
- Mushak 6.3: the **Download 6.3** button was an empty green pill because its Font Awesome icon no longer loads. The column now holds the invoice number and the report draws a Frappe button with the download icon and label; Excel exports get the invoice number instead of HTML.
