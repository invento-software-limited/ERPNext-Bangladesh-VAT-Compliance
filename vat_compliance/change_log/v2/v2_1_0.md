## [2.1.0] - 2026-10-19

### ✨ Added
- VAT Dashboard page (`/app/vat-dashboard`), linked as Dashboard after Home in the VAT Compliance sidebar: output, input and net VAT for the period, the VAT account balance as of today, a treasury-status strip (invoiced unpaid, collected, deposited, withheld by customers, VDS to deposit), output vs input by month, VAT postings by source, top customers and suppliers, and a paginated list of VAT postings that flags invoices without an Item Tax Template. Amounts come from postings on the VAT account(s); presets are This month, Last month, Fiscal year, 12 months and Custom.
- KPI and chart header above the table on all seven VAT Compliance reports (Sales VAT Management, VDS Management, Sales Register – Mushak 6.2, Purchase Register – Mushak 6.1, Mushak 6.3, Supplier Mushak 6.3, Purchase Sales Ledger For Trader). Every card is summed from the report's own rows; clicking a status, item type, supplier or period bar sets the report's filters. Status columns now show filled status pills.

### 🐞 Fixed
- VAT on invoices without an Item Tax Template was ignored. Sales VAT Management, VDS Management, Mushak 6.2, the trader ledger and the payment VDS deduction now read the line's `item_tax_rate` on the VAT account (Compliance Settings' default VAT account, or the company's VAT tax accounts) when no template is set; template lines are read exactly as before. Mushak 6.1 only counts a VAT-account rate on such lines.
- A withheld (VDS) payment against those invoices deducted ৳0; it now deducts the VAT due.
- Sales VAT Management dropped the VAT on credit notes, overstating output VAT; returns now net off.
- Mushak 6.3 always returned no rows: its query passed the date filters as if they were Sales Invoice fields. It now filters on posting date and reads invoices with the user's permissions.
- Sales VAT Management and VDS Management show their status legend inside the new header instead of a report message.
