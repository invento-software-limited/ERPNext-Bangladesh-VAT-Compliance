## [2.3.4] - 2026-11-05

### 🐞 Fixed
- VDS Management and VAT Payment: each tax group on a multi-rate payment now has its own deposit status and reference. Before, only the first group was offered for deposit, and one Journal Entry marked the whole payment as deposited.
- Deposit status now counts only submitted Journal Entries. A cancelled or draft entry no longer marks VAT as deposited, and a line already in a draft or submitted entry cannot be selected again.
- Sales VAT Management: an invoice with a cancelled payment no longer repeats or shows as unpaid. A sales payment on several VAT accounts now splits across them when deposited, where it used to stop with an error.
- Mushak 6.3, Supplier Mushak 6.3 and Mushak 6.6: Total VAT now counts VAT accounts only, so freight or other charges are no longer shown as VAT.
- Mushak 6.2 and the Purchase and Sales Ledger for Trader: VAT uses the rate saved on the invoice line, so editing a tax template cannot change past periods.
- Mushak 6.1, Mushak 6.2, the trader ledger, Sales VAT Management and VDS Management: VAT and values on foreign-currency invoices are reported in company currency. Part-payment shares also handle a party account in a different currency.
- VAT Payment and VAT Dashboard: a report that fails now shows an error, where it used to show zero VAT due.
- Mushak 6.3 Download All: the zip includes a manifest of included and failed invoices, and its name starts with `INCOMPLETE_` when any invoice failed.
- VAT Dashboard: ledger rows before the comparison period are summed into one row, so loads no longer fetch every VAT posting ever made.

### 🛡 Security
- `make_journal_entry` on VAT Payment and VDS Management: the server rebuilds amounts, accounts and company from the invoice and ignores those values from the browser. It also checks access to the payment and company, and refuses rows from more than one company.
- BIN and TIN status on Customer and Supplier: Verified is accepted only after a successful check on the server, and is reset when the number changes.

### 🔧 Changed
- Payment Entry: warns when VDS is withheld on a service whose Item Tax Template is marked as not subject to deduction. The amount does not change.
- `required_apps` now lists ERPNext, and `requests` and `beautifulsoup4` are declared as dependencies.
- Code formatting and lint fixes across the app, so the repo's pre-commit hooks pass.
