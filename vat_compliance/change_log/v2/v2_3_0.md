## [2.3.0] - 2026-11-05

### ✨ Added
- POS Sales Invoices now create VAT challans. Consolidated invoices are skipped, because each POS Invoice has its own challan.
- A daily VAT challan alert to System Managers and Accounts Managers covers invoices pending over 24 hours, failed, or stuck syncing, and a blank sync schedule when the integration is in use.
- BIN No field on Company, used by Mushak 6.6.
- Accounts Manager and Accounts User can now work with VAT Deduction Certificates and the VAT Challan reports. Accounts Manager can edit VAT Invoices and Accounts User can read them.
- Tests for challan sync, returns, permissions, VDS, Mushak 6.6 and stock balances, and a test step in CI.

### 🔧 Changed
- **POS challans are sent in a background job after the sale commits**, so a slow or failing vendor no longer blocks or undoes a sale or refund. A receipt printed straight away may not show the challan number.
- **Withheld VDS on part payments is based on the invoice's gross total**: paying 57.50 of a 115 invoice withholds 7.50. Pending tax lead approval.
- **Mushak 6.6 requires a BIN on both the Supplier and the Company.** Pending tax lead approval.
- VDS deductions are rebuilt from the current allocations when a draft Payment Entry is validated. Write-offs and other manual deductions are kept.
- POS Vendor Configuration: Sync Schedule is required and defaults to After Submit, and the Base URL must be HTTPS on an approved vendor host. Approve other hosts with `vat_challan_allowed_hosts` in site config.
- Mushak 6.1 and 6.2 load stock movements in one query instead of one per row.

### 🐞 Fixed
- Mushak 6.6 listed every supplier's withheld VAT instead of the named vendor's, and failed on invoices with several tax groups. **Review any certificates already issued.**
- Challan payloads: the line discount was deducted twice, invoice discounts were ignored, and timestamps were 6 hours late, which could move late-night sales into the wrong date or month.
- Scheduled VAT sync jobs failed because of a wrong job path.
- Sync no longer re-sends a sale the vendor already holds. A retry asks the vendor first, and invoices left in Syncing by a rejection or worker crash now become Failed.
- POS returns: the original sale is no longer sent again, a rejected return stays Failed instead of showing as returned, discounts are correct, and an unmatched line is an error rather than being dropped.
- Sales VAT Management showed the invoice's full VAT on each part payment.
- Mushak 6.1 and 6.2 opening and closing balances now carry from row to row, and each stock movement counts once.

### 🛡 Security
- Permission checks on endpoints that were open to any logged-in user: VAT certificate generation, VAT Invoice sync and download, vendor token and master sync, Mushak 6.3 and challan evidence uploads, VAT Payment data, and BIN/TIN checks. State-changing endpoints are POST-only, and BIN/TIN checks are limited to 30 a minute.
- Retailer document upload can only send files attached to that Retailer, so `site_config.json` and other site files can never reach the vendor.
- Owner NID and the vendor access token are stored encrypted, and a patch converts existing values.
- Vendor XML is parsed with `defusedxml` and a size limit, and vendor errors no longer reach users.
- Escaped party and invoice values on the VAT Payment page and Mushak 6.6 to prevent stored XSS.
- Removed five broken whitelisted Retailer wrappers.
